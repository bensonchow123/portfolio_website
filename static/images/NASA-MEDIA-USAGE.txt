created under NASA contract and STScI asserts no copyright over it either.
Credit is requested rather than legally required, and is given above.

NASA's name, insignia and logos are protected separately and are not used
here, since that could imply the agency endorses this site.

ESA republishes some of the same Webb images under CC BY 4.0 through
esawebb.org, which is a licence rather than a dedication. These were taken
from webbtelescope.org, so the public domain terms above are the ones that
apply.

Photographs of the site author are not NASA material and are not covered by
any of this.
